You can delete your HookPilot account yourself at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

## How to delete

1. Sign in and go to [/account](/account).
2. In the delete section, read the list of what will be deleted.
3. Click the delete button, enter your current password and confirm with **Delete everything permanently**.

You are signed out and returned to the home page.

## What is deleted

- Your login, all your sessions and your workspace memberships.
- Every workspace you **alone** own, with everything in it: all endpoints, captured requests, replay destinations and replay history. The account page lists these workspaces by name before you confirm.
- Pending invitations of those workspaces, and invitations you sent.
- Any active subscription of those workspaces. It is canceled at once, without a refund for the rest of the period.

## What is not deleted

- Workspaces that have another owner. You are removed from them, but the workspace and its endpoints stay for the other members.
- Invoices and payment records, which stay with our payment provider, Stripe, as required for accounting.
- Anonymous endpoints you created without being signed in are not part of your account. They expire on their own (at most 7 days after creation), or you can delete them in the inspector from the browser that created them.

## Before you delete

- If teammates rely on a workspace you alone own, they lose access to all its data when you delete your account.
- If you only want to stop paying, cancel the subscription instead. See [Billing, plan changes and cancellation](/help/billing-and-cancellation).
- If you only want to remove captured data, you can delete single requests, all requests of an endpoint, or whole endpoints. See [How long HookPilot keeps your data](/help/data-retention).

## Can't sign in to delete?

If you have forgotten your password, reset it first at [/forgot-password](/forgot-password). For other data requests, open a ticket from [/support](/support).
