Every plan includes all cleaning steps, CSV and XLSX in and out, and ZIP downloads for batches. On every plan your files are processed in your browser and never uploaded. Prices are in US dollars per month. The current plans are also shown on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Free: $0

- Files up to 5 MB
- 50,000 rows per file
- 3 files per batch
- Workflows saved in this browser and exported as JSON (none in your account)
- 1 team seat

The Free plan works without an account.

## Pro: $12 per month

- Files up to 50 MB
- 500,000 rows per file
- 20 files per batch
- 50 workflows saved in your account
- 1 team seat

## Business: $29 per month

- Files up to 200 MB
- 2,000,000 rows per file
- 100 files per batch
- Unlimited workflows in your account, shared with your team
- 5 team seats
- Priority email support

## How the limits are applied

- **File size, rows per file and files per batch** apply to processing in your own browser. The cleaner reads your plan's limits when the page loads and shows them in the drop area, for example "Free plan: up to 3 files, 5.0 MB and 50,000 rows each".
- **Saved workflows and team seats** are stored by DataPolish and enforced on our servers.
- Some safety caps apply on every plan, including Business, to stop the browser tab running out of memory (for example 256 MB per file). See [File formats and limits](/help/file-formats-and-limits).

## Checking your plan and usage

Signed in, open [Account → Billing](/account/billing). It shows your current plan, its file, row and batch limits, and meters for workflows saved in your account and team seats used.

## Changing plans

Only the workspace owner can change the plan. See [Billing and cancellation](/help/billing-and-cancellation).

When you move to a smaller plan, nothing is deleted:

- Account workflows over the new limit become read-only (apply, export or delete only).
- Team members over the new seat count keep access, but new invitations are blocked until you remove members or upgrade.
