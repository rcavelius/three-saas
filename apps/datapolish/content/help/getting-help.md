## Start with the help center and guides

- The [help center](/help) explains how the cleaner, workflows, plans, billing and accounts work.
- The [guides](/guides) go deeper into common data problems: fixing Excel dates, removing duplicates, currency and number formats, merging exports, CSV encoding in Excel, and cleaning a folder of exports every month.
- The [pricing page](/pricing) lists the current plans, limits and billing questions.
- The [privacy page](/privacy) explains what is and is not stored.

## Ask the support assistant

Go to [support](/support) and ask your question in plain words. The assistant answers from the articles in this help center. It can explain how a feature works, what a message means and which plan fits your files.

The assistant cannot see your files (they never leave your browser), and it cannot change your account, your plan or your billing.

## Open a ticket

If the assistant cannot help, or your request needs a person, open a ticket from [support](/support). You can follow your tickets at [/support/tickets](/support/tickets).

To get a faster answer, include:

- the email address of your account (if you have one) and your plan
- what you were trying to do and the exact message you saw
- your browser and operating system
- the file type and roughly how large it is (rows and columns)

Please do not paste sensitive spreadsheet contents into a ticket. Describe the problem or use a small made-up example instead.

## What a person handles

These requests always go to a person through a ticket:

- **Refunds**: decided case by case. There is no automatic refund.
- **Billing disputes** and questions about a specific charge.
- **Security reports**, if you think you have found a vulnerability or see activity on your account you do not recognise.
- **Legal questions**.
- **Data requests** about the account data we store.

Many things you can do yourself right away:

- change your plan, card or cancel at [Account → Billing](/account/billing) (owner only)
- manage teammates at [Account → Team](/account/team)
- change your password, sign out devices or delete your account at [Account](/account)
- reset a forgotten password at [Forgot password](/forgot-password)
