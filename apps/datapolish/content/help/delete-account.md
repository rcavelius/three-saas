You can delete your account yourself at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

## How to delete your account

1. Sign in and open [Account](/account) (the **Profile & security** tab).
2. Scroll to **Delete account**.
3. Enter your password.
4. Tick "I understand this permanently deletes my account and the data listed above."
5. Click **Delete my account**.

If the password is wrong, you see "Password is incorrect." and nothing is deleted. When deletion succeeds you are taken back to the home page.

## What is deleted or cancelled

Deleting your account immediately:

- **cancels any paid subscription** of workspaces you own alone. There are no further charges, but the current period is not refunded automatically.
- **deletes those workspaces**, their members' access, pending invitations and every workflow saved in them.
- **removes you from teams owned by someone else.** Workflows you saved there stay with the team, without your name.
- **deletes your login, sessions and profile** (name, email address and password hash).
- **unlinks your usage statistics** from you.

## What is not affected

- **Your spreadsheets.** They were never uploaded or stored, so there is nothing to delete.
- **Workflows saved in this browser.** They live in your browser's local storage and stay there. You can delete them in the **Saved workflows** panel, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
- **Files you downloaded** stay on your computer.

## Before you delete

- Export any account workflows you want to keep with the export button in the **Saved workflows** panel. Exported `.datapolish.json` files can be imported again later, even without an account.
- If you own a team workspace that others use, deleting your account deletes that workspace and its shared workflows for everyone, unless the workspace has another owner.
- If you only want to stop paying, you can cancel your plan instead and keep using the Free plan. See [Billing and cancellation](/help/billing-and-cancellation).

## Refunds and other data requests

If you want to ask about a refund for the current period, or have another request about your data, [contact support](/support). These requests are handled by a person. More about what we store is in [Security and privacy](/help/security-and-privacy) and the [privacy page](/privacy).
