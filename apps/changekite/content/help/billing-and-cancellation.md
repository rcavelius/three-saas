Billing is self-service from [Plan & billing](/account/billing). Payments are handled by Stripe on its own secure pages; ChangeKite never sees or stores your card number.

Only the **workspace owner** can change the plan or payment details. Admins and members see the current plan but not the buttons.

## Upgrading

1. Open [Plan & billing](/account/billing).
2. Choose **Upgrade to Solo** or **Upgrade to Agency**.
3. Complete the payment on Stripe's checkout page.

You return to Plan & billing afterwards. The new plan appears as soon as Stripe confirms the payment, usually within seconds; reload the page if it has not changed yet. If you leave checkout without paying, nothing is charged.

You can also start from the [pricing page](/pricing). If you are not signed in, you are asked to create an account first and are taken on to checkout after confirming your email.

## Changing plan, card or invoices

Once you have a subscription, Plan & billing shows **Manage subscription, payment method or cancel**. It opens Stripe's billing page, where you can:

- switch between Solo and Agency
- update your payment method
- see your invoices
- cancel your subscription

Upgrades apply immediately; the difference is prorated on the next invoice. A downgrade keeps your current plan until the end of the period you have already paid for.

## Cancelling

Cancel from the Stripe billing page described above. Your plan stays active until the end of the paid period, and Plan & billing shows the date it ends. After that the workspace moves to the Free plan:

- monitored sites are paused, not deleted
- free one-off scans keep working
- upgrading again later resumes paused sites, up to the new plan's limit

Scan history follows the history period of your current plan, so after moving to Free older scans are removed. The latest scan of each site is always kept.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, Plan & billing shows a warning and, where available, the date until which your plan stays active. Update your payment method on the Stripe billing page to keep your plan.

## One-time passes

The Full-site report pass is bought from a report or the [pricing page](/pricing), with or without an account. It is charged once, never renews and ends by itself after 7 days, so there is nothing to cancel. If a pass did not unlock what the pricing page describes, email support within 7 days of purchase for a full refund; a refunded pass stops working.

## Refunds and billing questions

If you were charged in error, or ChangeKite did not work for you in your first 14 days on a paid plan, ask within 14 days of the charge and a person will refund it in full, back to the original payment method through Stripe. Other refund requests are reviewed by a person, case by case. To ask, open a support ticket from [Support](/support) and include your workspace name. See [Getting help](/help/getting-help) and the [terms](/terms).

Note that deleting your account cancels any subscription of workspaces you own alone immediately, without a refund for the rest of the period. See [Deleting your account](/help/delete-your-account).
