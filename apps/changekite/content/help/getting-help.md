## Start with the help center

Most questions are answered in the [help center](/help). Good places to start:

- [Getting started with ChangeKite](/help/getting-started)

For how each check works, see [What we check](/docs) and the [guides](/guides).

## Ask the support assistant

On [Support](/support) you can ask the support assistant a question in your own words. It answers from the articles in this help center, so it can explain how scans, monitoring, plans, billing, teams and account settings work, and point you to the right page.

The assistant answers questions; it does not make changes to your account or issue refunds. If it cannot answer, or your question needs a person, open a ticket.

## Open a support ticket

From [Support](/support) you can open a ticket for a person to handle. You can follow your tickets on the [tickets page](/support/tickets).

To get a faster answer, include:

- your workspace name (shown at the top of the Team and Plan & billing pages)
- the address of the site involved, if any
- the report link, if your question is about a specific report
- what you expected to happen and what happened instead, with the exact error message

Never send your password or card details in a ticket.

## What a person handles

Some requests are always handled by a person, not by the assistant:

- **Refunds**: each request is reviewed case by case. We cannot promise a particular outcome.
- **Billing disputes** and questions about specific charges or invoices.
- **Security reports**: if you think you have found a security problem in ChangeKite, describe it in a ticket.
- **Legal questions**, including questions about the [terms](/terms) and [privacy policy](/privacy).
- **Data requests**, such as asking for a copy of your data or for deletion when you can no longer sign in.
