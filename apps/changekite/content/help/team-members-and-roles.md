Every account comes with a personal workspace, and you are its owner. A workspace holds the plan, the monitored sites, their reports and alerts. You manage people on the [Team](/account/team) page.

## Seats per plan

- **Free**: 1 seat
- **Solo**: 1 seat
- **Agency**: 5 seats

Members and pending invitations both use a seat. The Team page shows how many seats are in use. When all seats are taken you cannot send more invitations until you remove someone, cancel an invitation, or upgrade to Agency.

## Roles

- **Member**: sees every monitored site and report of the workspace, and can use **Scan now**.
- **Admin**: everything a member can do, plus adding and removing sites, changing monitoring settings (pause, mute, alert recipients), inviting people, removing members and cancelling invitations.
- **Owner**: everything an admin can do, plus changing other people's roles and managing the plan and billing.

Only owners can upgrade, change plans, update payment details or cancel.

## Inviting someone

1. Open [Team](/account/team). You need to be an owner or admin.
2. Under **Invite someone**, enter their email address and choose **member** or **admin**.
3. Choose **Send invitation**.

The invitation is emailed to them and is valid for 7 days. You can cancel a pending invitation from the Team page. Inviting the same address again replaces the earlier invitation.

## Accepting an invitation

1. Open the link in the invitation email.
2. Sign in, or create an account, using **the same email address the invitation was sent to**. A new account must confirm its email first; after confirming, open the invitation link again.
3. Choose **Join the workspace**, or **Decline**.

If accepting fails, the invitation may have expired or the workspace may have no free seats. Ask the person who invited you to send a new one.

## Changing roles and removing people

- Owners can change any other person's role between member, admin and owner using the role menu on the Team page. You cannot change your own role.
- Owners and admins can remove other members with **Remove**.

A workspace can have more than one owner. Giving someone else the owner role is a good idea before you delete your own account, because a workspace you own alone is deleted with your account. See [Deleting your account](/help/delete-your-account).
