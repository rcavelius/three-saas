You can delete your account yourself at any time. Deletion is permanent and immediate, and it cannot be undone.

## How to delete your account

1. Sign in and open [Account](/account).
2. Scroll to **Delete account** at the bottom of the page.
3. Read the list of what will be removed. If you own workspaces alone, they are named there.
4. Enter your current password.
5. Tick **I understand this cannot be undone**.
6. Choose **Delete my account**.

You need your password to delete the account. If you have forgotten it, reset it first from [Forgot your password?](/forgot-password).

## What is deleted

- Your login, your profile and every signed-in session.
- Every workspace where you are the **only owner**, together with all its monitored sites, scan history, alerts, team members and pending invitations.
- Your membership in workspaces that have another owner. Those workspaces and their data stay with their owners.
- Invitations you sent.

## Subscriptions

Any paid subscription of a workspace you own alone is cancelled at once, without a refund for the rest of the period. If you want to keep the data or the subscription, first make another member an owner on the [Team](/account/team) page; that workspace then stays with them.

If you only want to stop paying but keep your account, cancel the subscription instead. See [Billing, plan changes and cancellation](/help/billing-and-cancellation).

## Free report links

Reports from free scans you ran without signing in are not linked to your account, so deleting the account does not remove them. They expire on their own after the retention period (90 days by default).

## Something went wrong

- **"That password is not correct."** Check the password and try again. Repeated attempts are rate limited for a short time.
- If you can no longer sign in and need your data deleted, open a ticket from [Support](/support). Data requests are handled by a person.
