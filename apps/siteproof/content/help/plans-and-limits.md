SiteProof has three plans. Prices are in US dollars, billed monthly; taxes may apply where required. You can compare them on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Free: $0

- One-off scans from the home page, no account needed
- Up to 25 pages per scan
- Shareable report link
- Print to PDF from your browser
- No monitored sites, scheduled scans, email alerts or report history
- 1 team seat

## Solo: $19/month

- 3 monitored sites
- Up to 250 pages per scan
- Automatic re-scan every 24 hours
- Email alerts only when something changes
- 90 days of report history
- 1 team seat

## Agency: $79/month

- 20 monitored sites
- Up to 1,000 pages per scan
- Automatic re-scan every 6 hours
- Email alerts only when something changes
- 365 days of report history
- 5 team seats
- Server-generated PDF reports (up to 30 PDF exports per workspace per hour)
- Priority scan queue

On Free and Solo, use the **Print / PDF** button on a report to save it as a PDF from your browser.

## Seeing your usage

[Plan & billing](/account/billing) shows your current plan and status, how many monitored sites and team seats are in use, pages per scan, scan frequency, report history and whether PDF reports are included.

## Limits on free scans

To keep the scanner fair and stop it being used to flood websites, free scans have extra limits:

- A number of scans per hour from the same connection (5 per hour by default).
- A number of scans of the same website per hour, no matter who asks (10 per hour by default).
- A limit on how many scans run at the same time. If all scanners are busy, try again in a minute.

## What happens when you hit a plan limit

- **Sites**: when all your monitored sites are in use, adding another one is refused. Remove a site or upgrade.
- **Seats**: members and pending invitations both count towards your seats. When they are all used, you cannot send new invitations.
- **After a downgrade or cancellation**: your oldest sites, up to the new limit, stay active. Newer ones are paused (marked as paused by plan), never deleted. Upgrading again resumes them.

See [Billing, plan changes and cancellation](/help/billing-and-cancellation) for how to change plans.
