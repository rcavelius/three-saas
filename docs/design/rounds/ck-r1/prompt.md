You are a senior art director at a top web studio. Give me a design direction for the marketing site of a small SaaS called ChangeKite (changekite.com), then generate image mockups.

PRODUCT: ChangeKite is a website health scanner and monitor. It writes a plain-English report with a transparent 0-100 score (100 minus 10 per critical, 3 per warning, 1 per notice, each capped) and a grade: "In good order", "Needs attention", "Serious problems". Paid plans re-scan sites on a schedule and email only when something meaningful changes (site down, new broken link, certificate about to expire).

AUDIENCE: small-business site owners, marketers, freelancers and agencies looking after several sites. Not developers.

CURRENT SITE: warm paper background, near-black ink, Fraunces serif headings, one dark navy accent. It looks like a tasteful printed document, but plain and dated. Structure: header nav (What we check, Tools, Guides, Pricing, Help, Log in, Sign up); hero with H1 "Find what's broken on your site before your visitors do." + paragraph + URL input "Scan" form; a numbered list of the 12 checks; a "What a free scan is, and isn't" section; footer. Other pages: /tools (free single-purpose checkers), /guides, /pricing (two paid plans), auth, dashboard.

TREND PICKS (from Figma's 2026 web trends): bold typography, a controlled vibrant palette, motion design / scrollytelling, light gamification (score dial + grade only), sustainable web design (fast on mobile, no WebGL, no hero photos).

CONSTRAINTS: no fake testimonials, customer logos or invented stats. The hero visual must be the real product: a live-looking scan report. Must stay distinct from a dark developer-tool look (a sister product uses that). Must hit good mobile Lighthouse scores and support reduced motion. Built with Next.js + Tailwind v4 and Google fonts.

PLEASE GIVE ME:
1. Concept name and one-paragraph idea.
2. Type pairing (Google Fonts), sizes and tracking for H1/H2/body/labels.
3. Palette with hex values (background, surfaces, ink, accent, plus pass/warn/fail status colours) and contrast notes.
4. Layout for the home page, section by section, desktop and phone.
5. Motion plan (what moves, durations, easing), and what happens under reduced motion.
6. Hero idea in detail, including the product visual.
7. Three things that would make this feel award-level rather than template SaaS.
